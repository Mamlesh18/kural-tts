# Voice Recording, Model Training and Open Release Agreement (TEMPLATE)

> **This template is not legal advice.** It lists the points the agreement must cover for an
> open-source, commercially usable voice model. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction (India: Indian
> Contract Act, Copyright Act 1957, DPDP Act 2023) adapt it before anyone signs.

**Parties:** `[Project owner / entity]` ("Producer") and `[Voice talent legal name]` ("Talent").
**Persona name:** the synthetic voice will be published as **"Sahithi"**. The Talent's legal name
is **[not / optionally]** disclosed publicly (Talent's choice: ☐ anonymous ☐ credited as `[name]`).

## 1. What the Talent agrees to
1. Record about `[15]` hours of scripted Tamil, English and Tamil-English speech across
   `[~20]` sessions, scheduled by mutual agreement.
2. Allow the Producer to use the recordings to **train machine-learning models** that generate
   speech in a voice resembling the Talent's.

## 2. Open release, read carefully
3. The Producer will publish the trained model ("Voice Model") **publicly as open source**.
   **Anyone in the world** may download it and generate new speech in this voice, **including for
   commercial purposes**, under the model license in Section 4.
4. **Once published, the Voice Model cannot be recalled.** Copies will exist outside the Producer's
   control. The Talent understands that withdrawal after publication cannot remove those copies.
5. The **raw recordings** ☐ will not be published (default) ☐ will be published under `[license]`.

## 3. Compensation
6. Fee: `[amount]` (☐ one-time buy-out ☐ session fee + `[bonus/royalty]`), paid `[schedule]`.
   The fee reflects the perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable nature of the open release.

## 4. Protections for the Talent
7. The Voice Model is released under a **responsible-use license** that permits commercial use but
   prohibits:
   - impersonating the Talent or presenting generated audio as the Talent's own statements;
   - fraud, phishing, scams, and deceptive robocalls;
   - sexual content and harassment, and political campaigning or election content `[adjust]`;
   - unlawful uses, and use without disclosure where the law requires disclosure of synthetic media.
8. Generated audio carries an inaudible **watermark** by default where technically feasible.
9. The model card and README publish a **contact and takedown address** for misuse reports.
   The Producer will act on misuse within the Producer's control (e.g. hosted demos, its own
   products, and reporting license violations).
10. The Producer will not use the Talent's real name, photo or likeness to market the Voice Model
    without separate written consent.

## 5. Before publication
11. The Talent may withdraw consent **before public release** by written notice. The Producer
    will then delete the recordings and any models trained on them within `[30]` days. Fees for
    completed sessions remain payable.
12. The Talent will receive the release candidate audio samples `[14]` days before publication.

## 6. Data handling
13. Recordings are stored encrypted with access limited to `[named roles]`, and are not shared
    with third parties except processors needed for training (e.g. cloud GPU providers) under
    confidentiality.
14. Personal data is processed under applicable data protection law (India: DPDP Act 2023).

## 7. Ownership
15. Copyright and related rights in the recordings: `[assigned to Producer / licensed exclusively /
    licensed non-exclusively]` for the purposes above.
16. The Talent keeps all rights to their natural voice and to work outside this project.
